I built EastWire, a free site that puts news from China, India, South Korea and Japan on a
map, with every source shown. [link]

The hardest part wasn't code. It was permission. Before showing a single headline I read
the terms of 54 news publishers. Only 6 allow it. Every South Korean outlet I checked said
no, so the site tells you that coverage is thin there instead of filling the gap with
something it has no right to show.

A few things I'm proud of:
• Every story says where it came from and why it's on a city: mentioned, datelined, or
  where the publisher is. Country-only stories are never pinned to a capital.
• Sector tags (economy, tech…) come from published rules, not AI, and each tag shows the
  rules that fired. On a held-out test they were 100% precise but caught only a third of
  stories, and the site says so.
• Social posts from a handful of verified newsrooms sit in their own labelled section and
  load nothing from the platform until you ask.
• It runs for $0: Java/Spring Boot, Postgres, React, an hourly GitHub Actions job.
